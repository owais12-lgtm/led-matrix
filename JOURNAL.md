## June 8th: grrrr pcbs

- This started out with me basically stalking a bunch of GitHub repos from other people who'd made LED matrices before. Learned that people use ICs to increase their GPIO and drive more LEDs.
- Worked on making an LED matrix of my own, using through-hole LEDs — not risking SMD soldering with a Pinecil.
- Quickly finished the schematic and added footprints for the LEDs.
- Routed everything using both layers so I didn't need too many vias.
- Added a small silkscreen for extra whimsy, but there's honestly not much space for silkscreens.
